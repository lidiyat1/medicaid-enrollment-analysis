# medicaid-enrollment-analysis
# Georgia Medicaid Enrollment Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes Georgia Medicaid enrollment composition, coverage continuity, and beneficiary stability using SQL and Tableau with CMS-informed Medicaid data.

The analysis focuses on:
- Enrollment composition
- Medicaid vs CHIP participation
- Coverage continuity and churn
- Enrollment stability across eligibility groups
- Geographic distribution across Georgia counties

---

## Tools Used
- PostgreSQL
- Tableau
- Excel

---

## Project Structure
- `members.csv` – beneficiary demographic data
- `enrollment.csv` – enrollment history and plan participation
- `cms_summary.csv` – CMS enrollment summary statistics
- `medicaid_analysis_queries.sql` – SQL analysis queries
- `dashboard_preview.png` – Tableau dashboard screenshot

---

## Key Analyses
### Enrollment Composition
Analyzed Medicaid vs CHIP participation across enrolled beneficiaries.

### Coverage Continuity
Identified reenrollment patterns and multiple enrollment periods to evaluate potential coverage churn.

### Enrollment Stability
Calculated average enrollment duration across eligibility groups to compare beneficiary stability.

### Geographic Distribution
Evaluated Medicaid member distribution across Georgia counties.

---

## Key Findings
- Low-income adults and children represented a large share of enrolled beneficiaries.
- Enrollment stability varied across eligibility groups.
- Multiple enrollment periods suggested evidence of coverage churn and reenrollment activity.

---

## Recommendations
- Monitor populations with shorter enrollment duration for potential coverage instability.
- Support outreach efforts for reenrolling beneficiaries.
